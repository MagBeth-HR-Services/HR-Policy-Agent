# Horizon Technologies Policy Corpus Plan

## Purpose

Create a coherent, entirely fictional HR policy corpus for the Horizon Technologies HR Policy Agent.

The corpus will contain 11 documents in Markdown and PDF formats, totaling approximately 43 pages. All content will be original and contain no real company or employee information.

## Required Coverage

The documents will collectively cover:

- Remote, hybrid, and office-based work
- Temporary domestic and international work locations
- Information and data security
- Business expenses and reimbursement
- Company equipment
- Employee benefits
- Employee leave
- Onboarding
- Workplace conduct and escalation

## Citation Metadata

Every document will include:

- Stable document ID
- Document title
- Version number
- Effective date
- Policy owner
- Section numbers
- Section headings

Retrieved chunks will later preserve:

- Document ID
- Title
- Section number
- Section heading
- Page number when available
- Source filename
- Supporting snippet

## PTO Workflow Evidence

The PTO demonstration will primarily use:

- POL-001 Paid Time Off Policy
- POL-002 Company Holiday Policy when dates overlap with holidays
- Synthetic employee profile and PTO-balance records

The policies must support decisions concerning:

- PTO eligibility
- Available balance
- Minimum notice
- Manager approval
- Consecutive-day limits
- Conflicting holiday dates
- Requests that require HR escalation

## Remote-Work Workflow Evidence

The international remote-work demonstration will primarily use:

- POL-003 Remote Work Policy
- POL-004 Temporary Work Location Policy
- POL-005 Information Security Policy
- Synthetic employee profile and work-location records

The policies must support decisions concerning:

- Employee eligibility
- Requested duration
- Domestic versus international locations
- Manager and HR approval
- Tax, immigration, or legal escalation
- Information-security requirements
- Restricted data and equipment use
- Cases that cannot be automatically approved

## Document-Writing Rules

Each policy will:

- Apply only to the fictional Horizon Technologies company.
- Use clear, consistent language and section numbering.
- Include enough detail to answer realistic employee questions.
- Avoid contradictory rules unless the distinction is intentional and explained.
- Clearly identify exceptions and escalation requirements.
- Avoid real names, organizations, addresses, and private information.
- Avoid claiming to provide legal, tax, or immigration advice.
- Be reviewed for consistency with the other policies.

## Format Strategy

Nine documents will be stored as Markdown files.

Two documents will be converted to PDF:

- POL-003 Remote Work Policy
- POL-005 Information Security Policy

Using both Markdown and PDF will allow the ingestion pipeline to demonstrate support for at least two source formats.

## Completion Criteria

The policy corpus is complete when:

- All 11 planned documents exist.
- The total length is approximately 30-120 pages.
- Both Markdown and PDF ingestion can be demonstrated.
- Each document contains stable citation metadata and numbered sections.
- Both selected agentic workflows have sufficient policy evidence.
- At least one evaluation question requires evidence from multiple documents.
- No real private or improperly licensed information is included.
